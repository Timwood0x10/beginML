## 2. The Temporal View: Optimal Transport and Hamiltonian Dynamics (Dynamics & Transport)

**Core idea**: learning isn't instantaneous; it's the **physical evolution** of parameter particles on an **Energy Landscape**. This evolution follows the principle of least action or the optimal transport path.

**Corresponding chapters**:
*   [3. Optimization](3.Optimizer.md), [15. Diffusion](15.Diffusion.md), [22. Optimal Transport](22.OptimalTransport.md), [23. Game Theory](23.GameTheory.md)

### 2.1 Everything Is Flow
We can describe both optimization and generation with a unified **Stochastic Differential Equation (SDE)**:

$$
d\theta_t = \underbrace{-\mathbf{H}^{-1}\nabla \mathcal{L}(\theta_t) dt}_{\text{Gradient Flow}} + \underbrace{\mathbf{v}_{rot}(\theta_t) dt}_{\text{Hamiltonian Flow (Game)}} + \underbrace{\sqrt{2T} dW_t}_{\text{Diffusion Flow (Diffusion)}}
$$

1.  **Gradient flow (Ch 3, 19)**:
    *   This is the most basic downhill force.
    *   If the space is flat, $\mathbf{H}^{-1}=I$ (SGD).
    *   If the space is a Riemannian manifold, $\mathbf{H}^{-1}$ is the Fisher information matrix (Natural Gradient).
    *   **Optimal transport (Ch 22)** tells us this is essentially the **gradient flow** of probability distributions on Wasserstein geometry.

2.  **Hamiltonian flow (Ch 23)**:
    *   In GANs and multi-agent games, the **imaginary eigenvalues** of the Jacobian produce rotational forces.
    *   This causes spiral trajectories and the complexity of non-convex optimization (symplectic geometry).

3.  **Diffusion flow (Ch 15, 18)**:
    *   The noise $dW_t$ isn't a nuisance — it's the engine of exploration.
    *   It helps models escape saddle points and find **Flat Minima**, thereby guaranteeing generalization (Ch 7).

## 4. The Logical View: From Correlation to Causation (Logic & Causality)

**Core idea**: statistics can only tell us that "the rooster crows" correlates with "dawn breaks." Only causal inference can tell us that if you kill the rooster, dawn will still break. This is the necessary road to AGI.

**Corresponding chapters**:
*   [21. Causal inference](21.Causal.md), [16. Reinforcement Learning](16.RL.md)

### 4.1 Pearl's Causal Ladder
Today's deep learning mostly stays on the first rung, while mathematics is guiding us upward:

1.  **Association $P(y|x)$**:
    *   Observe.
    *   Tools: Curve Fitting, Bayes (Ch 2, 17).
    *   Status: LLMs are extremely good at this rung.
2.  **Intervention $P(y|do(x))$**:
    *   Act.
    *   Tools: Do-calculus, RL (Ch 16, 21).
    *   Status: Agent AI is exploring this rung.
3.  **Counterfactuals $P(y_x|x', y')$**:
    *   Imagine.
    *   Tools: Structural Causal Models (SCM).
    *   Status: the holy grail of AGI; machines need a mental model of "what if it had been different..."
